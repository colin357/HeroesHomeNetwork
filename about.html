About Heroes Home Network

A vetted network of real estate agents who are veterans and military spouses, matched to you by location and connected with you and your VA lender in one group chat. Built by people who have lived a PCS, for families who are about to.

Who We Are

What Heroes Home Network Is — and What It Is Not

Clarity starts with honesty about what this network does and does not do. Here is the straightforward answer.

What We Are

  • Veteran-led and VA focused: Every agent in the network is a veteran, current service member, or military spouse, verified with a DD-214, current orders, or a dependent ID before onboarding. Our lending team underwrites military income every day.
  • A location match, not a lead auction: You are matched to one accountable agent who works the market around your next duty station, with limited seats per installation so the match means a name, not a rotating roster.
  • One group chat: Once you are pre-approved, you, your agent, and your VA lender share a single thread so your closing date and your report date are the same conversation.
  • Education first: We publish plain-language guides, checklists, base and state PCS guides, and a free BAH calculator so you can make smart decisions without pressure.
  • Built for military reality: We cover PCS timing, BAH as qualifying income, Guard and Reserve income, occupancy rules, and the documentation hurdles that trip up families on a compressed timeline.

What We Are Not

  • Not a lead marketplace: We do not blast your information to multiple lenders or agents. One agent, one lender, one thread.
  • Not a call center: When you reach out, you talk to a VA loan specialist who reads LES statements daily, not a script and not a queue.
  • Not a rate table site: We do not publish best-rate tables or rank lenders based on paid placement.
  • Not a referral desk that sells ZIP codes: Agents earn a seat in the network by clearing our vetting standard. Nobody buys their way in.
  • Not a government agency: We are not affiliated with the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs, the Department of Defense, or any other government agency.
What We Do

How Heroes Home Network Helps Military Families

We focus on the parts of a PCS home purchase that cause the most confusion, and we are upfront about what we will not do.

Clarify eligibility

We help you understand entitlement basics and map a realistic path to pre-approval based on your report date, not a generic timeline.

Explain costs clearly

We walk through the funding fee, cash to close, seller concessions, and what a seller can and cannot be asked to pay, so there are fewer surprises.

Prepare for appraisal

We explain VA Minimum Property Requirements, the Tidewater process, and common fixes so you can plan early and avoid delays.

Research the duty station

Base guides, state PCS guides, and a BAH calculator so you know the market, the commute, and your housing budget before the movers arrive.

Match you with an agent

A pre-vetted agent who has PCS'd, or held the household together during one, and who has answered a listing agent's objection to a VA offer before.

What we will not do

No guarantees on rates, approvals, or timelines. No Social Security numbers or bank details on our standard forms. No pressure: use the guides and tools without applying, and walk away at any point. We do not sell your personal information.

Why We Built It

Why Heroes Home Network Exists

Heroes Home Network was built by people who have watched military families get straight answers about everything except the biggest purchase of a PCS. Orders arrive with two to four months of notice. The family explains BAH to one rep, uploads an LES for a second, and by the time an underwriter asks why the housing allowance is not on the W-2, they are on their fourth point of contact and nobody owns the report date.

The agent side is usually worse. A national referral desk hands you whoever paid for the ZIP code that month, often someone who has never written a VA offer and will quietly steer you toward conventional financing because it is easier for them.

The network was built to replace that with one agent who has lived a PCS, one lender who underwrites military income every day, and one thread where you can ask both of them a question at 2100 on a Tuesday. The mission is simple: explain the VA loan and PCS process clearly, set expectations early, and connect military families with vetted professionals who know the program and respect the family timeline.

How We Run the Network

What We Manage Day to Day

The goal is clear guidance, disciplined execution, and a process that respects the realities of military life when buying a home.

What we lead

  • Quality standards: We set expectations for service, ethics, and VA expertise, re-check every agent's closings, reviews, and license standing every 12 months, and remove partners who do not meet the bar.
  • Complex scenarios: We review difficult files involving manual underwriting, entitlement questions, or appraisal strategy so families are not left guessing.
  • Training: Every agent completes our VA loan class, and we keep documentation checklists and communication standards tight so you get clear answers and realistic timelines.

How that helps you

  • Fewer surprises: You understand the process, likely conditions, and true cash to close earlier in the timeline.
  • Faster clarity: When documents are ready, the conversation focuses on decisions and next steps, not back-and-forth confusion across four points of contact.
  • Better expectations: Edge cases are handled with honest guidance about what is possible, what is risky, and what to do next.
