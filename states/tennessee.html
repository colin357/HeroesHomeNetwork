PCSing to Tennessee

No state income tax and affordable markets from Clarksville to Millington.

Major installations in Tennessee

Fort Campbell (Clarksville)

Home of the 101st Airborne straddling the Kentucky line; Clarksville is one of the most affordable fast-growing cities in the country.

NSA Mid-South (Millington)

Navy personnel commands north of Memphis, where Tipton County suburbs offer low prices and short commutes.

Aerospace testing complex in middle Tennessee with small-town housing costs and easy access to Chattanooga and Murfreesboro.

Taxes for Military Families in Tennessee

Tennessee has no state income tax — military pay, retirement pay, and VA disability are all untaxed at the state level, and the old Hall tax on investment income has been fully repealed. Property taxes are among the lowest in the nation. Disabled veterans meeting eligibility criteria can receive property tax relief on a substantial portion of their home's value. Sales taxes are high, but for homeowners the overall burden is light.

Tennessee Veteran Benefits Worth Knowing

  • No state income tax on military pay, retirement pay, or SBP payments
  • Property tax relief on the primary residence for qualifying 100% disabled veterans and surviving spouses
  • Free hunting and fishing licenses for 100% disabled resident veterans
  • HiRE and veterans education benefits, including in-state tuition for veterans using the GI Bill

The Tennessee Housing Market for Military Buyers

Clarksville is a standout VA-loan market: heavy new construction keeps median prices well below the national average, and an E-5's BAH at Fort Campbell typically covers a new-build payment. Millington and Tipton County offer similar value near NSA Mid-South, with Memphis suburbs like Arlington and Bartlett a pricier step up. Around Arnold AFB, Tullahoma and Manchester remain genuinely inexpensive. Strong Guard and retiree demand keeps resale liquidity healthy statewide.

Top School Districts & Counties in Tennessee

  • Near Fort Campbell: Clarksville-Montgomery County schools serve most families; some buy across the line for Christian County, KY convenience
  • Near NSA Mid-South: Tipton County (Munford, Atoka, Brighton) is the classic military pick over Millington city schools
  • Memphis suburbs: Arlington, Lakeland, and Bartlett municipal districts rank among the metro's best
  • Near Arnold AFB: Coffee County and Tullahoma City schools, with Franklin County an option to the south

Weather & Climate in Tennessee

Middle and West Tennessee see regular severe weather, including tornadoes — Clarksville took a direct hit in December 2023 — so many buyers value basements or interior safe rooms, which are not standard in newer slab construction. Summers are humid with highs in the 90s°F, making HVAC condition a key inspection item. Winters are short and mild with occasional ice storms. Spring rains can stress drainage; check grading and crawlspace moisture on older homes.

Traffic & Commutes in Tennessee

Clarksville's growth has outpaced its roads: Highway 41A and Wilma Rudolph Boulevard clog at shift change, so buying near Exit 1 or on the Sango side can add real time to a Fort Campbell gate run — many families deliberately buy close to Gate 4 corridors. Commuting to Nashville down I-24 is a 45-plus-minute proposition. Millington commutes are easy, with US-51 handling Tipton County traffic smoothly. Around Arnold AFB, congestion is essentially nonexistent.

Buying in Tennessee with a VA loan: $0 down and no PMI apply everywhere in the state.
