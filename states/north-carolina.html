Taxes for Military Families in North Carolina

North Carolina does not tax military retirement pay for retirees with five or more years of service as of August 1989 (Bailey settlement) or 20+ years of service. Disabled veterans receive a property tax exclusion on the first $45,000 of home value with a 100% P&T rating.

North Carolina Veteran Benefits Worth Knowing

  • Military retirement pay exempt from state income tax for qualifying retirees
  • Property tax exclusion for 100% P&T disabled veterans
  • Robust veteran employment pipeline around Fort Bragg's defense sector
  • In-state tuition for veterans using the GI Bill

The North Carolina Housing Market for Military Buyers

Fayetteville and Jacksonville routinely appear on 'most affordable military towns' lists — an E-4's BAH can cover a mortgage on a three-bedroom home. Strong PCS turnover makes both markets popular for buy-then-rent strategies.

Buying in North Carolina with a VA loan: $0 down and no PMI apply everywhere in the state. Estimate your allowance with the BAH calculator, follow the PCS checklist, and read the full VA Loan Guide before you go under contract. Benefit details change — verify specifics with the state's department of veterans affairs.
