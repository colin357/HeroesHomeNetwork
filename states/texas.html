PCS State Guide

PCSing to Texas

No state income tax and some of the strongest veteran property tax exemptions in the country.

Duty Stations

Major installations in Texas

Fort Cavazos (Killeen)

One of the largest Army posts in the world. Killeen-Temple area median home prices remain well below the national average.

Fort Bliss (El Paso)

Massive training installation with some of the most affordable housing of any major duty station.

Joint Base San Antonio

Lackland, Randolph, and Fort Sam Houston. San Antonio blends big-city amenities with a moderate cost of living.

Naval Air Station Corpus Christi & Fort Hood region

Coastal and central Texas options with strong rental demand from military families.

Taxes for Military Families in Texas

Texas has no state income tax — military pay, retirement pay, and VA disability all go untaxed at the state level. Property taxes are higher than average, but veterans with a 100% VA disability rating receive a total property tax exemption on their homestead, and partial exemptions scale from 10% ratings up.

Texas Veteran Benefits Worth Knowing

  • Total homestead property tax exemption for 100% disabled veterans; partial exemptions from 10%+
  • Hazlewood Act: up to 150 hours of tuition exemption at Texas public universities
  • Texas Veterans Land Board home and land loan programs (stackable with a VA loan)
  • No state income tax on military retirement or SBP payments

The Texas Housing Market for Military Buyers

Texas offers some of the best BAH-to-mortgage math in the country. In Killeen and El Paso, an E-5's BAH commonly covers the full payment on a median single-family home. San Antonio and Austin-adjacent markets run higher but remain far below coastal price points.

Buying in Texas with a VA loan: $0 down and no PMI apply everywhere in the state. Estimate your allowance with the BAH calculator, follow the PCS checklist, and read the full VA Loan Guide before you go under contract. Benefit details change — verify specifics with the state's department of veterans affairs.
