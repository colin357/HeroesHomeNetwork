Taxes for Military Families in Texas

Texas has no state income tax — military pay, retirement pay, and VA disability all go untaxed at the state level. Property taxes are higher than average, but veterans with a 100% VA disability rating receive a total property tax exemption on their homestead, and partial exemptions scale up from 10% ratings.

Texas Veteran Benefits Worth Knowing

  • Total homestead property tax exemption for 100% disabled veterans; partial exemptions from 10%+
  • Hazlewood Act: up to 150 hours of tuition exemption at Texas public universities
  • Texas Veterans Land Board home and land loan programs (stackable with a VA loan)
  • No state income tax on military retirement or SBP payments

The Texas Housing Market for Military Buyers

Texas offers some of the best BAH-to-mortgage math in the country. In Killeen and El Paso, an E-5's BAH commonly covers the full payment on a median single-family home. San Antonio and Austin-adjacent markets run higher but remain far below coastal price points.

Top School Districts & Counties in Texas

  • Near JBSA: Comal ISD and Boerne ISD (Comal & Kendall Counties, north of San Antonio) and Alamo Heights ISD are perennial standouts
  • Near Fort Hood: Salado ISD and Belton ISD in Bell County are the local favorites over Killeen ISD
  • Near Fort Bliss: the west-side Franklin High School feeder pattern in El Paso ISD rates highest
  • Check GreatSchools and TEA accountability ratings for the specific feeder pattern — district quality varies block by block

Weather & Climate in Texas

Expect long, hot summers (95°F+ is routine June–September) and short, mild winters nearly everywhere. Central Texas (Killeen, San Antonio) sits in a hail-prone corridor — roof age matters when you buy. The Gulf Coast carries hurricane exposure and higher insurance costs, while El Paso is high desert: dry air, 300+ sunny days, and cool nights.

Traffic & Commutes in Texas

Killeen and El Paso commutes are easy — most gates are reachable in 15–25 minutes from popular neighborhoods. San Antonio is the exception: I-35, US-281, and Loop 1604 back up at rush hour, so many JBSA families buy near their assigned base (Lackland vs. Randolph are on opposite sides of the metro — confirm your gate before you pick a neighborhood).

Buying in Texas with a VA loan: $0 down and no PMI apply everywhere in the state. Estimate your allowance with the BAH calculator, follow the PCS checklist, and read the full VA Loan Guide before you go under contract. Benefit and district details change — verify specifics with the state's department of veterans affairs and school ratings sites.
