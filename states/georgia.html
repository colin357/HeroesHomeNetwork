PCS State Guide

PCSing to Georgia

Low cost of living, growing retirement pay exclusions, and five major installations.

Duty Stations

Major installations in Georgia

Fort Moore (Columbus)

Home of the Infantry and Armor schools, with one of the lowest-cost housing markets of any major post.

Fort Stewart & Hunter AAF (Savannah/Hinesville)

The largest Army installation east of the Mississippi, near historic Savannah.

Fort Eisenhower (Augusta)

The Army's cyber center of excellence, in a market where BAH stretches far.

Robins AFB (Warner Robins)

Massive logistics hub in middle Georgia with famously affordable housing.

Taxes for Military Families in Georgia

Georgia offers a military retirement income exclusion (up to $17,500, doubling with earned income, for retirees under 62 — plus standard retirement exclusions at 62+). Disabled veterans receive a substantial homestead exemption (roughly $100,000+ of assessed value, adjusted annually).

Georgia Veteran Benefits Worth Knowing

  • Homestead property tax exemption for qualifying disabled veterans
  • Military retirement pay exclusions from state income tax
  • Free driver's licenses and discounted licenses for veterans
  • Strong defense-sector employment in Columbus, Augusta, and Warner Robins

The Georgia Housing Market for Military Buyers

Georgia is one of the strongest value plays in military real estate: in Columbus, Augusta, and Warner Robins, median home prices sit far below the national average while BAH remains solid — junior enlisted families routinely buy here on a single income.

Buying in Georgia with a VA loan: $0 down and no PMI apply everywhere in the state. Estimate your allowance with the BAH calculator, follow the PCS checklist, and read the full VA Loan Guide before you go under contract. Benefit details change — verify specifics with the state's department of veterans affairs.
