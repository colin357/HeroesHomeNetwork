Taxes for Military Families in Maryland

Maryland taxes income on a progressive schedule plus county piggyback taxes, but military families get carve-outs: a portion of military retirement pay is exempt (a larger exclusion for retirees 55 and older), and overseas/combat pay receives favorable treatment. VA disability is untaxed. The standout benefit is property tax: veterans with a 100% permanent service-connected rating pay no state or local property tax on their primary residence, and recent law extended partial exemptions to certain lower ratings in participating counties.

Maryland Veteran Benefits Worth Knowing

  • Full property tax exemption on the primary residence for 100% permanently disabled veterans (transferable to surviving spouses)
  • Military retirement income subtraction, expanded for retirees age 55+
  • Edward T. Conroy Memorial Scholarship for qualifying veterans and dependents at Maryland public colleges
  • Free admission and license benefits at state parks for disabled veterans

The Maryland Housing Market for Military Buyers

Maryland is a higher-cost, high-stability market. Around Fort Meade, Odenton, Severn, and Hanover command premium prices but rent and resell reliably thanks to NSA-driven demand — many families house-hack the strong rental market when they PCS. Andrews buyers find comparative value in southern Prince George's and Charles County. Pax River is the affordability standout: St. Mary's County offers single-family homes at prices that would be townhouse money near the Beltway. VA loan limits are rarely an issue outside the immediate D.C. suburbs.

Top School Districts & Counties in Maryland

  • Howard County Public Schools: consistently among the top-ranked systems in the country and a Fort Meade favorite (Columbia, Elkridge)
  • Anne Arundel County: Severna Park and Crofton feeder zones draw Meade and Annapolis-area families
  • Near JB Andrews: many families choose Calvert County or Charles County schools over closer-in options
  • Near Pax River: St. Mary's County schools, with Leonardtown zones the most sought-after

Weather & Climate in Maryland

Maryland is four-season with humid 90°F summers and winters that swing from mild to the occasional nor'easter dumping heavy snow. The Chesapeake Bay drives the biggest home-buying issue: flood zones and rising insurance costs in waterfront and low-lying communities around Annapolis, southern Anne Arundel, and St. Mary's County — always pull the flood map before falling for a water view. Remnant hurricanes bring wind and rain most years. Basements are standard; check sump pumps and grading, since humid summers make moisture management a real inspection item.

Traffic & Commutes in Maryland

Traffic is the tax Maryland charges on top of its taxes. Fort Meade sits between the Baltimore and D.C. commutes — MD-32 and the B-W Parkway crawl at rush hour, so Odenton and Severn's short hops beat anything involving I-95. Andrews families should respect the Beltway: living south in Clinton or Waldorf trades MD-5 congestion for cheaper housing. Pax River is the exception — MD-235 moves, and most of Lexington Park and Leonardtown is within 25 minutes of the gate.

Buying in Maryland with a VA loan: $0 down and no PMI apply everywhere in the state. Benefit and district details change — verify specifics with the state's department of veterans affairs and school ratings sites.
