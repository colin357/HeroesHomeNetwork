PCSing to Hawaii

Paradise duty with the nation's highest BAH rates, generous property tax breaks, and no tax on military retirement.

Duty Stations

Major installations in Hawaii

Joint Base Pearl Harbor-Hickam (Honolulu)

Navy and Air Force hub on Oahu's south shore, where Ewa Beach and Kapolei are the main military buying corridors.

Schofield Barracks (Wahiawa)

25th Infantry Division's home in central Oahu, with Mililani the perennial favorite for Army buyers.

Windward Oahu station where Kaneohe and Kailua offer stunning settings at premium prices.

Pohakuloa Training Area (Hawaii Island)

High-altitude training area on the Big Island, where supporting personnel find far cheaper housing in Hilo and Waimea.

Taxes for Military Families in Hawaii

Hawaii exempts military retirement pay from state income tax, and VA disability is untaxed; active-duty pay is taxable for Hawaii residents, though most stationed members keep their home-state residency. The offset to high prices is that Hawaii has the lowest effective property tax rates in the nation, and veterans who are totally disabled are exempt from property tax on their principal residence in most counties aside from minimal annual charges. Confirm county-specific rules with the local real property tax office.

Hawaii Veteran Benefits Worth Knowing

  • Property tax exemption on the principal residence for totally disabled veterans in most counties (small minimum tax may apply)
  • Full state income tax exemption on military retirement pay
  • Special disabled-veteran vehicle registration exemptions and license plates
  • Hawaii State Veterans Cemetery burial benefits and a state veterans home on the Big Island

The Hawaii Housing Market for Military Buyers

Oahu is expensive, but the math works better than sticker prices suggest: BAH rates are among the highest anywhere, effective property taxes are the nation's lowest, and VA loans remove the down-payment barrier. Ewa Beach, Kapolei, and Mililani are the core military ownership corridors, with strong resale to the next PCS wave. Watch for leasehold listings — always confirm a property is fee simple — and budget for condo fees and older plumbing in mid-century buildings.

Top School Districts & Counties in Hawaii

  • Statewide: Hawaii runs a single state school district, so individual school ratings matter more than district lines
  • Near Schofield Barracks: Mililani's complex of schools is the most consistent military favorite on Oahu
  • Near JB Pearl Harbor-Hickam: Ewa Beach and Kapolei-area schools serve the main buying corridors, with several newer campuses
  • Near MCB Hawaii: Kailua and Kaneohe schools serve windward families, and private-school demand is notably high islandwide

Weather & Climate in Hawaii

Temperatures barely move — roughly 75-88°F year-round — so heating and cooling costs are minimal, though trade-wind exposure determines whether you need AC at all (leeward Ewa and Kapolei are hotter than windward Kaneohe). The buyer-relevant risks are moisture and salt: expect faster roof, paint, and appliance wear near the coast, and inspect for termites, which are endemic. Windward and valley homes see heavy rain and occasional flooding, and hurricane insurance is a standard line item.

Traffic & Commutes in Hawaii

Oahu traffic is the island's biggest lifestyle tax. The H-1 westbound from Ewa Beach and Kapolei toward Pearl Harbor-Hickam crawls every morning — leaving 15 minutes late can cost 45. Mililani to Schofield via H-2 is the rare easy commute, one reason Army families cluster there. MCB Hawaii commuters over the Pali or Likelike from town face steady congestion, so most buy windward. Test-drive your exact commute at 0600 before writing an offer.

Buying in Hawaii with a VA loan: $0 down and no PMI apply everywhere in the state. Benefit and district details change — verify specifics with the state's department of veterans affairs and school ratings sites.
