PCS State Guide

PCSing to Ohio

Military pay and retirement are exempt from state income tax, anchored by one of the Air Force's largest bases at Wright-Patterson.

Duty Stations

Major installations in Ohio

Wright-Patterson AFB (Dayton)

Headquarters of Air Force Materiel Command and AFRL — one of the largest Air Force bases, surrounded by affordable Dayton-area suburbs.

Youngstown Air Reserve Station (Vienna)

The 910th Airlift Wing's home in the Mahoning Valley, one of the cheapest housing markets in the Midwest.

Rickenbacker ANGB (Columbus)

Guard airlift and refueling wings on Columbus's south side, within reach of a strong, growing metro job market.

Camp James A. Garfield JMTC (Ravenna)

Ohio National Guard's major training center in Portage County, between Akron and Youngstown.

Taxes for Military Families in Ohio

Ohio exempts active-duty military pay earned by residents stationed outside Ohio and fully exempts military retirement pay and SBP from state income tax. Nonresidents stationed in Ohio pay no Ohio tax on military pay. Watch municipal income taxes, though — Ohio cities levy their own income taxes, and rules for military pay vary by municipality. Property taxes are moderate, and the state's homestead exemption offers an enhanced reduction for 100% service-connected disabled veterans.

Ohio Veteran Benefits Worth Knowing

  • Full state income tax exemption on military retirement pay and SBP
  • Enhanced homestead property tax exemption for veterans with a 100% service-connected disability rating, without the income test
  • Ohio War Orphans and Severely Disabled Veterans' Children Scholarship for dependents' college tuition
  • Ohio National Guard Scholarship covering a large share of tuition at state schools for Guard members

The Ohio Housing Market for Military Buyers

The Dayton metro around Wright-Patterson is one of the country's better-value base markets: Beavercreek, Fairborn, and Huber Heights offer everything from starter homes to executive housing at prices below the national median, with strong rental demand from a rotating population of officers, civilians, and contractors. Youngstown-area homes near the Air Reserve Station are remarkably cheap. Columbus near Rickenbacker is hotter and appreciating fast, with new construction pushing south toward the base.

Top School Districts & Counties in Ohio

  • Near Wright-Patterson: Beavercreek City Schools (Greene County) is the traditional base favorite, with Bellbrook-Sugarcreek close behind
  • Oakwood and Centerville (Montgomery County): highly rated Dayton-area districts within a reasonable base commute
  • Near Rickenbacker: Canal Winchester and Pickerington (Franklin/Fairfield Counties) draw Guard and Reserve families
  • Near Youngstown ARS: Canfield and Poland (Mahoning County) pair strong schools with very low home prices

Weather & Climate in Ohio

Ohio delivers humid 90°F summers, gray winters with periodic snow and ice, and a real spring severe-weather season — the Dayton area was hit by significant Memorial Day tornadoes in 2019, so basements and roof age are worth attention at inspection. Northeast Ohio near Youngstown catches lake-effect snow off Lake Erie, adding winter maintenance. Freeze-thaw cycles are hard on driveways and foundations, and older housing stock statewide makes sewer-line and basement-moisture inspections money well spent.

Traffic & Commutes in Ohio

Wright-Patterson commutes are gate-dependent: Beavercreek and Fairborn addresses reach most gates in 10-20 minutes, while I-675 backs up at Colonel Glenn Highway and the Fairfield Road interchange at peak times. Huber Heights works well for Area A via Route 4. Rickenbacker sits near the I-270/US-23 corridor south of Columbus, where growth is outpacing the roads — Canal Winchester keeps it short. Youngstown ARS commutes off Route 11 are quick from anywhere in the Mahoning Valley.
