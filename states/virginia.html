Taxes for Military Families in Virginia

Virginia taxes income, but is phasing in a substantial military retirement pay subtraction ($40,000+ per year). Veterans with a 100% permanent and total service-connected disability rating receive a full property tax exemption on their primary residence — one of the strongest exemptions in the country, extended to surviving spouses.

Virginia Veteran Benefits Worth Knowing

  • Full real property tax exemption for 100% P&T disabled veterans and surviving spouses
  • Military retirement income subtraction
  • Virginia Values Veterans (V3) hiring program
  • In-state tuition for veterans and dependents using GI Bill benefits

The Virginia Housing Market for Military Buyers

Hampton Roads (Norfolk, Virginia Beach, Chesapeake) is a perennial military buy-and-hold market — huge rental demand from constant PCS turnover. Northern Virginia is expensive but pairs with the highest BAH rates in the continental U.S.

Top School Districts & Counties in Virginia

  • Northern Virginia: Fairfax County, Loudoun County, and Arlington run some of the highest-rated public schools in America (with home prices to match)
  • Hampton Roads: York County is the quiet standout for Langley-Eustis families; Virginia Beach and Chesapeake city schools rate well for Norfolk-area sailors
  • Quantico: Stafford and Prince William Counties balance commute and school quality
  • Virginia school quality is county-run — buying one county over can change your assigned schools entirely

Weather & Climate in Virginia

Four real seasons: humid 90°F summers, colorful falls, and winters that bring occasional snow (more in NoVa, less on the coast). Hampton Roads carries hurricane and nor'easter exposure — flood zones and flood insurance are a genuine line item there, so check the FEMA map before you fall in love with a waterfront listing.

Traffic & Commutes in Virginia

Northern Virginia has some of the worst traffic in the nation — I-95, I-66, and the Beltway crawl at rush hour, and Pentagon/Belvoir families plan lives around HOV and Express lanes (or the VRE and Metro). In Hampton Roads, the water crossings rule everything: the HRBT and Midtown tunnels back up daily, so buy on the same side of the water as your duty station.

Buying in Virginia with a VA loan: $0 down and no PMI apply everywhere in the state. Estimate your allowance with the BAH calculator, follow the PCS checklist, and read the full VA Loan Guide before you go under contract. Benefit and district details change — verify specifics with the state's department of veterans affairs and school ratings sites.
