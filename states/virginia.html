PCSing to Virginia

The heart of East Coast military life — from the Pentagon to the world's largest naval base.

Duty Stations

Major installations in Virginia

Naval Station Norfolk

The largest naval base in the world. Hampton Roads is one of the most military-dense housing markets in America.

Fort Belvoir & the Pentagon

Northern Virginia offers unmatched career opportunity — and DC-area housing prices to match, offset by the nation's highest BAH bands.

Marine Corps Base Quantico

Crossroads of the Marine Corps, within commuting distance of the DC metro.

Joint Base Langley-Eustis

Air Force and Army together in Hampton, with affordable waterfront communities nearby.

Taxes for Military Families in Virginia

Virginia taxes income, but is phasing in a substantial military retirement pay subtraction ($40,000+ per year). Veterans with a 100% permanent and total service-connected disability rating receive a full property tax exemption on their primary residence — one of the strongest exemptions in the country, extended to surviving spouses.

Virginia Veteran Benefits Worth Knowing

  • Full real property tax exemption for 100% P&T disabled veterans and surviving spouses
  • Military retirement income subtraction
  • Virginia Values Veterans (V3) hiring program
  • In-state tuition for veterans and dependents using GI Bill benefits

The Virginia Housing Market for Military Buyers

Hampton Roads (Norfolk, Virginia Beach, Chesapeake) is a perennial military buy-and-hold market — huge rental demand from constant PCS turnover. Northern Virginia is expensive but pairs with the highest BAH rates in the continental U.S.

Buying in Virginia with a VA loan: $0 down and no PMI apply everywhere in the state. Estimate your allowance with the BAH calculator, follow the PCS checklist, and read the full VA Loan Guide before you go under contract. Benefit details change — verify specifics with the state's department of veterans affairs.
