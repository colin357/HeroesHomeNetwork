Taxes for Military Families in South Dakota

South Dakota has no state income tax, so military pay, retirement pay, and VA disability compensation are all untaxed at the state level. There is also no state inheritance tax. Property taxes are moderate, and disabled veterans can qualify for a partial property tax exemption on their dwelling, with paraplegic veterans eligible for a complete exemption.

South Dakota Veteran Benefits Worth Knowing

  • No state income tax on military pay, retirement pay, or SBP payments
  • Partial property tax exemption on the homes of qualifying disabled veterans; full exemption for paraplegic veterans
  • Reduced tuition at state universities for eligible veterans
  • State Veterans Bonus payments for qualifying periods of service

The South Dakota Housing Market for Military Buyers

The B-21 Raider mission is reshaping the Ellsworth market: Box Elder and east Rapid City are seeing new construction and rising demand ahead of thousands of incoming personnel, yet prices remain well below the national median. Buying early in a tour can position you well for appreciation. Sioux Falls offers a larger, steadier market with abundant inventory and some of the lowest cost-of-living figures of any growing metro.

Top School Districts & Counties in South Dakota

  • Near Ellsworth AFB: Douglas School District in Box Elder serves the base directly and is used to military moves
  • Rapid City: Rapid City Area Schools offers more building choice for families west of the base
  • Near Sioux Falls: Sioux Falls School District plus fast-growing suburban districts like Brandon Valley
  • Sioux Falls suburbs: Harrisburg and Tea Area districts are top picks in the metro's southern growth corridor

Weather & Climate in South Dakota

Hail is the headline risk near Ellsworth: the Black Hills region sees frequent severe hailstorms, so roof age drives both inspections and insurance premiums — impact-resistant shingles can earn discounts. Winters bring subzero stretches and blizzards, making insulation quality, furnace age, and garage access real considerations. Summers are pleasant, typically in the 80s°F. Rapid City sits in a mild banana belt by Dakota standards, while Sioux Falls gets the fuller brunt of prairie winters and wind.

Traffic & Commutes in South Dakota

Traffic is a minor factor statewide. Ellsworth commuters from Box Elder reach the gate in minutes, and even west Rapid City is only 15-20 minutes down I-90 — though winter storms can slow the interstate and make a shorter commute worth paying for. Watch for growth-driven congestion around the base as B-21 construction ramps up. In Sioux Falls, I-229 and 41st Street see rush-hour slowdowns, but almost any neighborhood keeps commutes under 20 minutes.
