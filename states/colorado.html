Taxes for Military Families in Colorado

Colorado has a flat state income tax with a generous military retirement exclusion (up to $24,000 depending on age). Disabled veterans with a 100% P&T rating qualify for a 50% property tax exemption on the first $200,000 of their home's value.

Colorado Veteran Benefits Worth Knowing

  • Property tax exemption for 100% P&T disabled veterans
  • Military retirement pay exclusion from state income tax
  • One of the largest veteran populations per capita in the U.S.
  • Strong VA infrastructure anchored by the Rocky Mountain Regional VA Medical Center

The Colorado Housing Market for Military Buyers

Colorado Springs BAH is among the highest for a non-coastal market, and the city's east and south sides still offer new-construction homes within an E-6 BAH budget. Denver-metro (Buckley) runs hotter — pre-approval before house hunting is essential.

Top School Districts & Counties in Colorado

  • Colorado Springs: Academy District 20 (near USAFA) and Cheyenne Mountain D12 are the marquee districts; Lewis-Palmer D38 in Monument is worth the short I-25 commute
  • East side: Falcon D49 pairs newer, affordable neighborhoods with quick Peterson/Schriever gates
  • Buckley / Denver metro: Cherry Creek Schools and Douglas County are the standard picks
  • El Paso County alone has a dozen districts — match the neighborhood to the district before you write an offer

Weather & Climate in Colorado

Around 300 days of sunshine a year, low humidity, and four seasons with drama: snow arrives and melts fast, spring brings hail (a leading cause of roof claims — check roof age), and summer days run warm with cool evenings. You'll live at 6,000+ feet — give the family a couple of weeks to acclimate to the altitude.

Traffic & Commutes in Colorado

Colorado Springs commutes are reasonable — Powers Boulevard moves most military traffic to Peterson and Schriever, and Fort Carson's gates pull from the south side. I-25 through the metro slows at rush hour but rarely gridlocks. Buckley families face genuine Denver congestion on E-470 and I-225; Aurora neighborhoods close to the base save real time.

Buying in Colorado with a VA loan: $0 down and no PMI apply everywhere in the state.
