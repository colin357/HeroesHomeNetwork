PCSing to Alaska

No state income tax, an annual Permanent Fund Dividend, and unmatched outdoor access for military families.

Major installations in Alaska

Joint Base Elmendorf-Richardson (Anchorage)

JBER anchors Anchorage, where inventory is tight and well-kept homes near base move fast in the summer PCS season.

Eielson Air Force Base (Fairbanks/North Pole)

F-35 growth has tightened the North Pole and Fairbanks markets, making early house-hunting essential.

Interior Alaska Army post where many families weigh on-post housing against Fairbanks' compact civilian market.

Taxes for Military Families in Alaska

Alaska has no state income tax and no state sales tax, so military pay, retirement pay, and VA disability are all untouched at the state level (some boroughs levy local sales tax). Residents who meet the physical-presence rules also receive the annual Permanent Fund Dividend. Disabled veterans rated 50% or higher receive a property tax exemption on the first portion of assessed value of their primary residence — verify the current cap with your borough assessor.

Alaska Veteran Benefits Worth Knowing

  • Property tax exemption on a primary residence for veterans with a 50%+ disability rating (verify current assessed-value cap)
  • Annual Permanent Fund Dividend for qualifying residents, including military members claiming Alaska residency
  • Alaska Housing Finance Corporation Veterans Mortgage Program with preferential rates, stackable with a VA loan
  • Veteran land purchase and discount programs through the state Department of Natural Resources

The Alaska Housing Market for Military Buyers

Anchorage is a supply-constrained market — buildable land is limited and new construction is expensive, so solid homes near JBER see multiple offers each PCS season. Eagle River is the classic military compromise of value and commute. In the Interior, North Pole and Fairbanks prices are lower, but inspect heating systems, insulation, and foundations carefully; a cheap house with an old boiler is not cheap at 40 below. High BAH rates generally keep the math workable.

Top School Districts & Counties in Alaska

  • Near JBER: Anchorage School District's Eagle River and South Anchorage attendance zones are the usual military picks
  • Matanuska-Susitna Borough: Palmer and Wasilla schools draw families trading commute for space and price
  • Near Eielson and Fort Wainwright: Fairbanks North Star Borough School District, with North Pole schools popular for Eielson families
  • Kodiak: Kodiak Island Borough School District serves the entire Coast Guard community

Weather & Climate in Alaska

Climate is the biggest home-buying variable in Alaska. Anchorage winters are snowy but moderated by the coast; Fairbanks routinely hits -30°F and colder, so heating fuel type, boiler age, insulation, and arctic entries directly drive monthly costs. Ask about ice damming, roof snow load, and permafrost or frost-heave issues on foundations in the Interior. Summers bring near-endless daylight and mild temperatures, and earthquake insurance is worth pricing in Southcentral Alaska.

Traffic & Commutes in Alaska

Anchorage traffic is modest, but the Glenn Highway from Eagle River and the Mat-Su Valley into JBER is the one true chokepoint — winter accidents can double a Wasilla commute, so many families cap their search at Eagle River. On-base gates back up at shift change. In Fairbanks, the Richardson Highway between North Pole and Fort Wainwright or Eielson is quick, though ice fog and winter road conditions add time; Kodiak commutes are measured in minutes.

Buying in Alaska with a VA loan: $0 down and no PMI apply everywhere in the state. Estimate your allowance with the BAH calculator, follow the PCS checklist, and read the full VA Loan Guide before you go under contract. Benefit and district details change — verify specifics with the state's department of veterans affairs and school ratings sites.
