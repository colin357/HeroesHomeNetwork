PCS State Guide

PCSing to Mississippi

Tax-free military retirement, falling income tax rates, and the lowest home prices of almost any state with major installations.

Duty Stations

Major installations in Mississippi

Keesler AFB (Biloxi)

Air Force technical training hub on the Gulf Coast, where beach-adjacent living is possible on a military budget.

Columbus AFB (Columbus)

Pilot training base in the Golden Triangle, with some of the cheapest homes of any flying assignment.

NAS Meridian (Meridian)

Jet training base in east Mississippi surrounded by deeply affordable Lauderdale County housing.

Camp Shelby (Hattiesburg)

Major Guard and mobilization training site south of Hattiesburg, a growing college-town market.

Taxes for Military Families in Mississippi

Mississippi fully exempts military retirement pay — all retirement income is untaxed — and the state is phasing its flat income tax steadily downward, with the rate scheduled to keep falling. National Guard and Reserve pay is exempt, and VA disability is untaxed. Homeowners get a standard homestead exemption, and veterans with a 100% service-connected disability rating are fully exempt from property taxes on their homestead — one of the cleaner total exemptions in the South.

Mississippi Veteran Benefits Worth Knowing

  • Total homestead property tax exemption for 100% service-connected disabled veterans and unremarried surviving spouses
  • All retirement income, including military pensions and SBP, exempt from state income tax
  • In-state tuition for veterans and dependents at Mississippi public universities, plus a tuition program for children of certain deceased or disabled members
  • State income tax on wages continues to phase down for everyone, including working spouses

The Mississippi Housing Market for Military Buyers

Mississippi may offer the best raw affordability of any state guide we publish. Around Columbus AFB and NAS Meridian, median homes price so low that BAH often covers a 15-year note. The Gulf Coast near Keesler costs more and carries wind and flood insurance premiums that must be built into your budget — Ocean Springs and D'Iberville are the popular compromises between price, schools, and insurance zones. Rental demand from students at every base keeps exit options open when you PCS.

Top School Districts & Counties in Mississippi

  • Near Keesler AFB: Ocean Springs School District is the coast's most sought-after, with Biloxi and Gulfport districts serving closer-in neighborhoods
  • Near Columbus AFB: Lowndes County schools — especially the Caledonia attendance zone — are the clear military favorite
  • Near NAS Meridian: Lauderdale County schools, with many families choosing the Northeast Lauderdale zone
  • Near Camp Shelby: Lamar County (Oak Grove, Purvis) and Petal School District lead the Hattiesburg metro

Weather & Climate in Mississippi

Heat, humidity, and hurricanes drive the checklist. Summers run above 90°F for months, so AC systems and duct condition are must-inspect items statewide. On the coast near Keesler, wind and flood insurance can add hundreds per month — get quotes and elevation certificates before writing offers, and favor fortified or post-Katrina construction. Inland at Columbus and Meridian, hurricanes fade but tornadoes and severe hail take over; ask roof age and consider a safe room. Snow is a non-factor; termite and moisture inspections are not.

Traffic & Commutes in Mississippi

Commutes are short everywhere except coastal chokepoints. Around Keesler, the I-110 loop and Highway 90 through Biloxi back up in summer tourist season and casino hours, so Ocean Springs commuters should plan around the Biloxi Bay bridge. Columbus AFB sits north of town off US-45; Caledonia and New Hope living keeps drives under 20 minutes. NAS Meridian is rural — the commute question is simply how far out Highway 39 you want to live. Hattiesburg traffic near Camp Shelby is minimal outside US-49 rush.

Buying in Mississippi with a VA loan: $0 down and no PMI apply everywhere in the state. Estimate your allowance with the BAH calculator, follow the PCS checklist, and read the full VA Loan Guide before you go under contract. Benefit and district details change — verify specifics with the state's department of veterans affairs and school ratings sites.
