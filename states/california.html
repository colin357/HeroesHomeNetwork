Taxes for Military Families in California

California taxes military retirement pay as ordinary income under high marginal rates — a recent push to exempt it has not yet become law, so verify the latest before counting on relief. Active-duty pay is exempt for residents stationed outside California, and VA disability is untaxed. Proposition 13 caps property tax growth once you buy, and the Disabled Veterans' Property Tax Exemption shields a substantial, inflation-adjusted amount of assessed value for 100% disabled veterans, with a larger low-income tier.

California Veteran Benefits Worth Knowing

  • Disabled Veterans' Property Tax Exemption on a substantial share of assessed value for 100% disabled veterans (inflation-adjusted; verify current amounts)
  • CalVet Home Loan program offering competitive financing that can be used alongside or instead of a VA loan
  • College Fee Waiver covering tuition at CSU/UC and community colleges for dependents of qualifying disabled veterans
  • Proposition 13 caps annual assessed-value growth, stabilizing property taxes after purchase

The California Housing Market for Military Buyers

California is a BAH-math state: San Diego prices are steep, but the region's very high BAH plus VA zero-down financing keeps ownership within reach for many mid-career families, especially in Chula Vista, Santee, and Oceanside. Travis families find real value in Fairfield, Vacaville, and Suisun City. Ridgecrest near China Lake and Lemoore-area markets are genuinely cheap. Resale to incoming military families is a reliable exit strategy near every fleet concentration.

Top School Districts & Counties in California

  • Near Naval Base San Diego: Poway Unified and San Dieguito districts are the regional academic standouts; Chula Vista's Eastlake schools are the value pick
  • Near Camp Pendleton: Carlsbad Unified and San Marcos Unified draw officer and senior-enlisted families
  • Near Travis AFB: Vacaville Unified and Travis Unified (which serves on-base housing) are the usual choices in Solano County
  • Near China Lake: Sierra Sands Unified serves Ridgecrest and most station families

Weather & Climate in California

Coastal San Diego is arguably the best home-owning climate in America — minimal heating and cooling costs, though marine-layer moisture makes inspections for older roofs and drainage worthwhile. The bigger statewide issue is wildfire insurance: premiums and carrier availability vary block by block in Fallbrook, Vacaville hills, and other wildland-interface areas, so get an insurance quote before waiving contingencies. China Lake's high desert swings from 100°F+ summers to freezing nights, and earthquake risk statewide makes retrofit status worth asking about.

Traffic & Commutes in California

Traffic shapes value here. I-5 into Naval Base San Diego and the SR-52/I-15 corridors back up hard; Chula Vista via I-805 is often the most predictable commute to the waterfront. Camp Pendleton's main gate traffic makes Oceanside and Fallbrook far easier than commuting up from central San Diego. Travis families should note I-80's Bay Area spillover — Fairfield beats Vacaville at rush hour. Ridgecrest commutes to China Lake take minutes.

Buying in California with a VA loan: $0 down and no PMI apply everywhere in the state. Estimate your allowance with the BAH calculator, follow the PCS checklist, and read the full VA Loan Guide before you go under contract. Benefit and district details change — verify specifics with the state's department of veterans affairs and school ratings sites.
