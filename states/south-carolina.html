PCS State Guide

PCSing to South Carolina

Low property taxes, exempt military retirement pay, and affordable markets from Columbia to the coast.

Duty Stations

Major installations in South Carolina

Fort Jackson (Columbia)

The Army's largest basic training post; Columbia's suburbs offer solid homes well below the national median.

Shaw AFB (Sumter)

F-16 wing and Army Central headquarters in a small-city market with very low buy-in prices.

Joint Base Charleston

Air Force and Navy missions in a fast-growing coastal metro with strong appreciation but rising prices.

MCRD Parris Island & MCAS Beaufort

Marine Corps recruit training and air station in the Lowcountry, where Beaufort blends coastal charm with reasonable costs.

Taxes for Military Families in South Carolina

South Carolina fully exempts military retirement pay from state income tax. Owner-occupied homes are assessed at a favorable 4% ratio with school operating taxes largely removed, keeping effective property tax rates among the lowest in the nation. Veterans rated 100% permanently and totally disabled receive a full property tax exemption on their home and up to five acres.

South Carolina Veteran Benefits Worth Knowing

  • Military retirement pay fully exempt from state income tax
  • Total homestead property tax exemption for 100% permanently and totally disabled veterans
  • Free tuition at state colleges for children of certain qualifying veterans
  • In-state tuition rates for veterans, active-duty members, and their dependents

The South Carolina Housing Market for Military Buyers

Markets vary widely by base. Sumter and Columbia remain genuinely affordable, with an E-5's BAH comfortably covering median homes in most suburbs. Charleston is the outlier: strong job growth and coastal demand have pushed prices well above the state average, sending many military buyers to Goose Creek, Summerville, and Moncks Corner. Beaufort sits in between, with flood zones and insurance costs worth checking on any Lowcountry purchase.

Top School Districts & Counties in South Carolina

  • Near Fort Jackson: Richland District Two and Lexington-Richland Five are the go-to picks in the Columbia metro
  • Near Joint Base Charleston: Dorchester District Two (Summerville) and Berkeley County (Goose Creek, Cane Bay) draw heavy military demand
  • Near Shaw AFB: Sumter School District serves most families; some commute from Lexington County for its schools
  • Near Parris Island/MCAS Beaufort: Beaufort County schools, with Lady's Island and Habersham popular with Marine families

Weather & Climate in South Carolina

Hurricanes shape the buying decision on the coast: Charleston and Beaufort homes often sit in flood zones, so factor flood insurance and elevation certificates into any Lowcountry offer, and expect wind mitigation credits to matter on insurance quotes. Inland around Columbia and Sumter, the bigger issues are humid 95°F-plus summers — check HVAC condition — and occasional severe thunderstorms. Winters are mild statewide, with snow a rarity, which keeps heating costs and winter maintenance low.

Traffic & Commutes in South Carolina

Charleston has the state's toughest commutes: I-26 into the peninsula and the roads through Summerville back up daily, so many JB Charleston families buy in Goose Creek or Hanahan to shorten the gate run. Columbia's I-77 and I-20 interchanges slow at rush hour but rarely gridlock, keeping Northeast Columbia convenient to Fort Jackson. Sumter commutes to Shaw AFB along US-76 are quick, and Beaufort-area drives are short apart from bridge traffic to Lady's Island.
