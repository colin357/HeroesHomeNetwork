PCSing to Missouri

No state tax on military pay or retirement, paired with two low-cost duty stations where BAH stretches far.

Duty Stations

Major Army training post in the Ozarks, where Pulaski County homes price well below the national median.

Home of the B-2 stealth bomber wing; families split between tiny Knob Noster and the larger town of Warrensburg.

Taxes for Military Families in Missouri

Missouri is one of the friendlier tax states for service members: active-duty pay is 100% deductible from state taxable income, and military retirement pay is fully exempt. VA disability is untaxed. Property taxes are moderate, and former POWs with a 100% rating receive a full homestead exemption; other disabled veterans rely on the state's property tax credit rather than a broad exemption, so run the numbers rather than assuming Texas-style relief.

Missouri Veteran Benefits Worth Knowing

  • 100% deduction of active-duty military pay from Missouri taxable income
  • Military retirement pay and SBP fully exempt from state income tax
  • Wartime Veteran's Survivors Grant and in-state tuition policies at Missouri public colleges
  • Full property tax exemption for former POWs with a total service-connected disability; property tax credit available to other qualifying disabled veterans

The Missouri Housing Market for Military Buyers

Missouri's two big duty stations are textbook BAH-positive markets. Around Fort Leonard Wood, St. Robert and Waynesville homes routinely price under $250,000, and heavy PCS turnover keeps the rental market strong for owners who move on. Near Whiteman, Knob Noster offers gate-proximity while Warrensburg adds college-town amenities and inventory; many families also commute from Lee's Summit on the Kansas City side for bigger-city resale strength at a longer drive.

Top School Districts & Counties in Missouri

  • Near Fort Leonard Wood: Waynesville R-VI serves the post and surrounding Pulaski County with well-regarded military-student programs
  • Near Whiteman AFB: Knob Noster R-VIII covers base housing and the town at the gate
  • Warrensburg R-VI (Johnson County): the larger-town option 15 minutes from Whiteman with strong schools
  • Near Rosecrans ANG: St. Joseph School District in Buchanan County, with Savannah R-III a popular small-town alternative

Weather & Climate in Missouri

Missouri gets everything: 95°F humid summers, icy winters, and a serious spring severe-weather season with tornadoes and large hail — roof age and impact-rated shingles matter for both safety and insurance premiums, and basements or storm shelters add real value near Whiteman and Fort Leonard Wood. The Ozarks around Fort Leonard Wood see flash flooding along creeks, so check flood maps even well away from major rivers. Ice storms cause winter power outages; a generator transfer switch is a practical upgrade.

Traffic & Commutes in Missouri

Neither major base has real traffic. At Fort Leonard Wood, the only friction is morning gate lines off I-44 — St. Robert living puts you five minutes out. Whiteman commuters from Warrensburg take US-50 about 15 minutes; those trading up to Lee's Summit or Blue Springs accept a 45-60 minute drive on US-50, which is fine until winter ice hits. Rosecrans sits minutes from downtown St. Joseph across the Missouri River, with I-229 keeping commutes under 15 minutes citywide.
