Taxes for Military Families in Florida

Florida has no state income tax — military pay, retirement, and disability are untaxed. Veterans with a 100% P&T service-connected disability receive a full homestead property tax exemption; partial exemptions and combat-related discounts also apply. The standard homestead exemption and Save Our Homes cap benefit all resident homeowners.

Florida Veteran Benefits Worth Knowing

  • Full homestead property tax exemption for 100% P&T disabled veterans
  • No state income tax on any military income
  • Congressman C.W. Bill Young Tuition Waiver for out-of-state veterans
  • Discounted or free state park entry and license benefits for veterans

The Florida Housing Market for Military Buyers

Florida markets vary widely: Pensacola and Jacksonville remain reasonable, while Tampa and the Emerald Coast have appreciated quickly. High BAH in Tampa and Fort Walton Beach helps close the gap — run your numbers with our BAH calculator before house hunting.

Buying in Florida with a VA loan: $0 down and no PMI apply everywhere in the state. Estimate your allowance with the BAH calculator, follow the PCS checklist, and read the full VA Loan Guide before you go under contract. Benefit details change — verify specifics with the state's department of veterans affairs.
