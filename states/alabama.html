PCSing to Alabama

Low property taxes, no tax on military retirement, and some of the most affordable base towns in the country.

Taxes for Military Families in Alabama

Alabama fully exempts military retirement pay from state income tax, and VA disability compensation is untaxed. Active-duty pay is taxable for Alabama residents, though nonresidents stationed in-state pay nothing on military wages. Property taxes are among the lowest in the nation, and veterans rated 100% permanently and totally disabled qualify for a full homestead property tax exemption; seniors and certain disabled homeowners get additional relief. Verify current rules with your county revenue commissioner.

Alabama Veteran Benefits Worth Knowing

  • Full state income tax exemption on military retirement pay
  • Homestead property tax exemption for 100% permanently and totally disabled veterans
  • Alabama G.I. Dependent Scholarship Program covering tuition for dependents of qualifying disabled veterans
  • Discounted hunting/fishing licenses and free or reduced vehicle tags for certain disabled veterans

The Alabama Housing Market for Military Buyers

Alabama is one of the best BAH-arbitrage states in the country. Around Fort Novosel, an E-4's BAH can comfortably cover a median home in Enterprise or Ozark. Huntsville has appreciated quickly thanks to Redstone's growth, but median prices still sit below the national average, and property tax bills are tiny by national standards, which keeps monthly payments low even as prices rise.

Top School Districts & Counties in Alabama

  • Near Redstone Arsenal: Madison City Schools and the Huntsville City magnet programs are the regional standouts
  • Near Fort Novosel: Enterprise City Schools is the traditional favorite for aviation families
  • Near Maxwell AFB: Pike Road Schools and Autauga County (Prattville) draw most military buyers out of Montgomery proper
  • Near Anniston Army Depot: Oxford City Schools and Jacksonville City Schools lead Calhoun County

Weather & Climate in Alabama

Alabama summers are hot and humid, with highs in the 90s°F, so budget for real cooling costs and check HVAC age during inspections. The state sits in Dixie Alley: springtime tornadoes and severe hail are a genuine underwriting factor, so ask about roof age, wind mitigation, and whether a safe room or basement exists (many homes are slab-built). Winters are mild with rare snow, and flood zones along creeks near Huntsville and the Wiregrass deserve a flood-map check before closing.

Traffic & Commutes in Alabama

Huntsville commutes into Redstone Arsenal back up hardest at Gate 9 off I-565 and along Zierdt Road; buying in Madison or south Huntsville keeps most drives under 30 minutes. Fort Novosel commutes are easy — Enterprise and Daleville are minutes from the gates, while Dothan adds roughly 35 minutes. Maxwell AFB traffic is light by big-city standards, though I-65 through Montgomery slows at rush hour; Prattville commuters should test the drive before buying.

Buying in Alabama with a VA loan: $0 down and no PMI apply everywhere in the state.
