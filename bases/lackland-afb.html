Schools

Northside Independent School District

NISD is one of Texas's largest and best districts, serving most of Lackland's off-base population. It consistently outperforms the state on most metrics and has strong fine arts and athletic programs.

What families should know

  • O'Connor, Stevens, and Warren High Schools are flagship NISD secondaries
  • District-wide magnet and career academy programs for specialized interests
  • Exceptional fine arts pipeline feeding the San Antonio arts community
Things to Do

Life around Lackland AFB

Attraction

The Alamo

Texas's most iconic historic site in downtown San Antonio — a must-visit pilgrimage for every family stationed in the city.

Dining

San Antonio River Walk

The famous limestone-lined canal through downtown lined with restaurants, bars, and hotels — the heart of San Antonio's tourism and dining scene.

Attraction

Natural Bridge Caverns

Texas's largest known commercial cavern system north of San Antonio with spectacular formations, outdoor adventure tours, and a discovery village.

Entertainment

SeaWorld San Antonio

One of the country's top SeaWorld parks with rides, marine animal shows, and a full waterpark adjacent to the Lackland area.

Outdoors

Leon Creek Greenway

A paved multi-use trail along Leon Creek near the base perfect for cycling, running, and family outings.

Shopping

Market Square (El Mercado)

The largest Mexican market in the US outside of Mexico with artisan goods, authentic food, and cultural events in the heart of San Antonio.
