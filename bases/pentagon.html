Schools

Arlington Public Schools / Alexandria City Public Schools

Both Arlington and Alexandria run highly rated urban school systems with strong diversity and academic programs, familiar to DoD families.

What families should know

  • Arlington's three high schools consistently rank among Virginia's best
  • Alexandria's T.C. Williams (now Alexandria City High School) has a renowned performing arts program
  • Both districts have active military family liaison programs given the Pentagon's enormous workforce
Things to Do

Life around The Pentagon

Attraction

Washington DC National Mall

America's front yard — Smithsonian museums, the Lincoln Memorial, WWII Memorial, and more, all free and within Metro reach of the Pentagon.

Dining

Alexandria King Street

Old Town Alexandria's main street with dozens of acclaimed restaurants, wine bars, and locally-owned shops steps from the Potomac.

Entertainment

Kennedy Center for the Performing Arts

America's premiere performing arts venue across the river in DC — military discounts available and world-class performances year-round.

Outdoors

Mount Vernon Trail

18-mile paved trail along the Potomac from the Pentagon to Mount Vernon — one of the DC area's most popular running and cycling routes.

Shopping

Fashion Centre at Pentagon City

Upscale mall directly above the Pentagon City Metro station — the most convenient shopping destination for Pentagon workers.

Outdoors

Tidal Basin / Cherry Blossoms

Washington's iconic cherry blossom display around the Tidal Basin peaks in late March — a spectacular annual highlight for families stationed nearby.

Housing Market

Buying or renting near The Pentagon

Home Prices

$700,000–$1,400,000

Typical Rent

$2,600–$4,500/mo

Pentagon assignments are in the most expensive housing market in the country. BAH is at the maximum rate but purchasing is extremely difficult. Most junior officers rent; senior officials and flag officers typically receive quarters on post at Fort Myer.

Popular neighborhoods: Pentagon City, Old Town Alexandria, Ballston
