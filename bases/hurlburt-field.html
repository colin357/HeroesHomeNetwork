Schools

Santa Rosa County School District

Santa Rosa County schools serve the Navarre and Mary Esther areas near Hurlburt. The district consistently outperforms Florida averages and is one of the state's strongest, particularly in the Navarre area.

What families should know

  • Navarre High School is one of Florida's top-ranked public schools
  • District serves a highly educated and engaged military family population
  • Strong dual enrollment programs with Pensacola State College
Things to Do

Life around Hurlburt Field

Outdoors

Navarre Beach

One of Florida's most pristine and least-crowded beaches with sugar-white sand and Gulf access — a Hurlburt family staple.

Attraction

Air Force Special Operations Command Museum

An on-base museum tracing the history of USAF special operations aviation from WWII Carpetbaggers to modern AFSOC.

Outdoors

Soundside Park

A Santa Rosa Sound waterfront park popular for kayaking, fishing, and watching stunning sunsets over the sound.

Dining

Floyd's Shrimp House

A legendary Gulf Coast seafood restaurant serving fresh local shrimp, grouper, and oysters in a classic Florida fish shack setting.

Entertainment

The Track Family Fun Parks

A go-kart and family entertainment center on the Fort Walton Beach strip popular with military families for birthday celebrations.

Outdoors

Blackwater River State Park

One of the purest sand-bottom rivers in the world for kayaking, canoeing, and swimming through pristine Florida longleaf pine forest.
