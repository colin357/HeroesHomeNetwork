MacDill AFB

Air Force Tampa, Florida

Off-Base Living

Top neighborhoods near MacDill AFB

South Tampa (Ballast Point)

An upscale South Tampa neighborhood directly adjacent to MacDill's north gate with waterfront views, historic bungalows, and walkable streets.

5–10 min to main gate

Brandon

A large suburban community east of Tampa with strong schools, major retail, and more affordable housing than South Tampa. Very popular with MacDill families.

20–25 min to main gate

Riverview

A rapidly growing community south of Brandon with new master-planned communities, A-rated schools, and direct access to I-75 for the base commute.

Schools

Hillsborough County Public Schools

HCPS is Florida's third-largest district with highly variable quality by area. Schools in Brandon and Riverview are among the district's strongest. South Tampa magnet schools are also highly desirable.

What families should know

  • Riverview High School and Newsome High School in the FishHawk area are A-rated Florida schools
  • Hillsborough County offers choice programs including magnets and IB programs
  • The district's military family liaison office actively supports PCS transitions
Things to Do

Life around MacDill AFB

Outdoors

Tampa Riverwalk

A 2.6-mile waterfront park along the Hillsborough River through downtown Tampa connecting museums, restaurants, and Amalie Arena.

Entertainment

Busch Gardens Tampa

A world-class theme park and zoo hybrid just north of MacDill with major roller coasters, African wildlife, and a full waterpark.

Outdoors

Clearwater Beach

Ranked one of America's best beaches, the Gulf-side Clearwater Beach is about 45 minutes from MacDill with beautiful white sand and calm water.

Dining

Oxford Exchange

An acclaimed Tampa café and marketplace in a stunning historic building near the University of Tampa serving upscale breakfast and lunch.

Dining

Ybor City

Tampa's historic Latin quarter with a lively nightlife scene, Cuban sandwiches at La Segunda Bakery, and the Columbia Restaurant since 1905.

Outdoors

Hillsborough River State Park

A state park north of Tampa with kayaking, hiking, and some of Florida's rare rapids — a natural escape close to the city.
