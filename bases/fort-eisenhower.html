Schools

Columbia County School District / Richmond County School System

Columbia County (Grovetown, Evans, Martinez) is the preferred district for military families due to higher-performing schools and newer facilities.

What families should know

  • Grovetown High School and Evans High School serve the primary military family areas
  • Columbia County SD consistently outperforms state averages
  • District has a military family liaison program for incoming PCS families
Things to Do

Life around Fort Eisenhower

Outdoors

Augusta Canal National Heritage Area

Historic canal with hiking, biking, and boat tours along a 7-mile stretch — one of Augusta's best outdoor resources.

Attraction

Masters Golf Tournament (Augusta National)

One of golf's four majors, held every April at Augusta National Golf Club — a world-class event in the backyard of Fort Eisenhower.

Dining

Frog Hollow Tavern

Award-winning Southern cuisine restaurant in downtown Augusta, known for farm-to-table dishes and an impressive whiskey selection.

Outdoors

Riverwalk Augusta

Scenic promenade along the Savannah River in downtown Augusta with parks, festivals, and waterfront dining.

Shopping

Augusta Mall

The region's largest mall with over 100 stores and dining options, centrally located for military families.

Outdoors

Phinizy Swamp Nature Park

Free nature park with boardwalks and trails through wetlands, excellent for birdwatching and nature walks with kids.
