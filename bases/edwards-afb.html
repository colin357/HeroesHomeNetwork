Off-Base Living

Top neighborhoods near Edwards AFB

Rosamond

The closest off-base community to Edwards, Rosamond is an unincorporated Kern County community with very affordable homes and a strong military presence.

Schools

Antelope Valley Union High School District

Elementary schools in the Antelope Valley fall under multiple districts. The overall quality is mixed; many families with academic priorities seek out charter schools or accept the tradeoff for the unique Edwards experience.

What families should know

  • Eastside Union School District (Lancaster area) has several well-rated elementary schools
  • STEM-focused charter options are available in Lancaster and Palmdale
  • The Antelope Valley College dual enrollment program benefits high school students
Things to Do

Life around Edwards AFB

Attraction

Air Force Flight Test Museum

On-base museum chronicling the storied history of flight testing at Edwards, including Yeager's sound barrier aircraft and early space program capsules.

Outdoors

Poppy Reserve (Lancaster)

During peak bloom (March–April), the Antelope Valley California Poppy Reserve explodes in orange — one of California's most spectacular wildflower displays.

Outdoors

Vasquez Rocks Natural Area

Iconic tilted sandstone formations used in countless films and TV shows, with hiking trails and climbing in the hills south of Palmdale.

Dining

BLVD Kitchen & Cocktails

A popular upscale-casual restaurant in Lancaster serving creative American cuisine in a stylish setting on the Lancaster BLVD arts corridor.

Entertainment

Lancaster Performing Arts Center

A professional-grade venue hosting Broadway touring shows, concerts, and community theater events in the Antelope Valley.

Attraction

Mojave Air & Space Port

A civilian spaceport and aviation hub in nearby Mojave where experimental aircraft, Virgin Galactic, and aerospace startups test vehicles.
