Schools

Leavenworth USD 453

USD 453 serves Leavenworth and is familiar with the CGSC student population cycling through annually. The district has processes in place for frequent military family transitions.

What families should know

  • Leavenworth High School is the main campus for base families
  • Several elementary schools are rated above state average
  • Basehor-Linwood USD 458 is highly rated for families who can commute from the county
Things to Do

Life around Fort Leavenworth

Attraction

Fort Leavenworth National Cemetery

One of the nation's oldest military cemeteries, with guided tours offering deep insight into Army history from frontier days onward.

Attraction

Frontier Army Museum

On-post museum documenting the history of Fort Leavenworth and the frontier Army — highly relevant to CGSC students and history buffs.

Dining

Missouri River Brewery

Leavenworth craft brewery with river views, house-made beers, and a popular patio — a great spot to unwind after command school.

Outdoors

Weston Bend State Park

Scenic Missouri River park with hiking trails, views, and fall foliage — one of the region's most beautiful state parks.

Entertainment

Kansas City (Power & Light District)

KC's entertainment hub is just 30 minutes away, offering world-class dining, live music, Chiefs/Royals games, and the famous KC BBQ scene.

Shopping

Zona Rosa Shopping Center

Large outdoor lifestyle center in North Kansas City with national retailers — the closest significant shopping hub to Fort Leavenworth.
