Beale AFB

Air Force Marysville, California

Off-Base Living

Top neighborhoods near Beale AFB

Linda/Olivehurst

Unincorporated Yuba County communities directly adjacent to Beale AFB. Most base-adjacent housing is here — convenient but modest.

Marysville/Yuba City

The twin cities straddling the Feather River offer the best mix of services, dining, and housing for Beale families. Yuba City is especially family-friendly.

15–20 min to main gate

SuburbanFamily-friendlyWalkable

Grass Valley

A charming Gold Rush-era town in the Sierra Nevada foothills with excellent schools, boutique shops, and outdoor recreation. A longer commute but highly desirable.

35–45 min to main gate

HistoricFamily-friendlyRetiree-friendly
Schools

Marysville Joint Unified School District

MJUSD serves the core Beale AFB commute area. Yuba City Unified is also widely used and considered stronger academically. Families willing to commute to Grass Valley access Nevada County's well-regarded schools.

What families should know

  • Yuba City High School and River Valley High School offer strong AP programs
  • Several charter school options in Yuba-Sutter area
  • Proximity to Sacramento for additional educational resources
Things to Do

Life around Beale AFB

Outdoors

Sycamore Ranch County Park

A Yuba County park along the Yuba River with swimming holes, hiking trails, and picnic areas popular with Beale families during hot summers.

Attraction

Tribal Heritage Preserve (Yuba-Sutter)

A cultural site preserving and interpreting the heritage of the Maidu people of the Yuba-Sutter area.

Entertainment

State Theatre Marysville

A restored 1930s movie palace in historic downtown Marysville hosting films, concerts, and community events.

Dining

Taqueria La Hacienda

A popular Mexican restaurant in Yuba City serving authentic tacos, burritos, and aguas frescas — a local institution.

Attraction

Empire Mine State Historic Park

One of California's richest gold mines turned state park in Grass Valley with mine tours, trails, and a Victorian cottage museum.

Outdoors

Feather Falls Scenic Area

A 9.5-mile round-trip hike to one of the tallest waterfalls in the continental US, located in the Plumas National Forest east of Oroville.
