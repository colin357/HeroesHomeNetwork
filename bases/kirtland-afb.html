Schools

Albuquerque Public Schools

APS is New Mexico's largest district with highly variable school quality. Families near Kirtland often seek charter schools or private options. The Rio Rancho Independent School District is widely regarded as better than APS.

What families should know

  • Eldorado High School (APS) is among the district's strongest academic performers
  • Rio Rancho Public Schools is consistently rated higher than APS overall
  • National Academy Foundation programs at several APS high schools
Things to Do

Life around Kirtland AFB

Outdoors

Sandia Peak Tramway

The world's longest aerial tramway rises 4,000 feet to the 10,378-ft Sandia Peak crest with panoramic New Mexico views and skiing in winter.

Attraction

Balloon Fiesta Park

Home to the Albuquerque International Balloon Fiesta every October — the world's largest hot air balloon event with 500+ balloons.

Attraction

Old Town Albuquerque

The historic heart of the city with Spanish colonial architecture, adobe galleries, Native American jewelry, and the New Mexico Museum of Natural History.

Dining

Range Café

A beloved Albuquerque institution with multiple locations serving New Mexican comfort food, green chile stew, and famous desserts.

Outdoors

Petroglyph National Monument

Thousands of ancient Native American petroglyphs carved into West Mesa basalt — a unique cultural and hiking destination right in the city.

Outdoors

Bosque Trail System

Miles of paved and unpaved trails through the Rio Grande cottonwood forest offering birding, cycling, and equestrian access.
