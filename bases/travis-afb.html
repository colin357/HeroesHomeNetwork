Travis AFB

Air Force Fairfield, California

Off-Base Living

Top neighborhoods near Travis AFB

Fairfield

The city immediately outside Travis AFB with diverse neighborhoods, good shopping, and quick base access. Offers a mix of affordable and mid-range housing options.

5–10 min to base

Military communitySuburbanDiverse

Vacaville

A growing city north of Fairfield with excellent schools, premium outlet shopping, charming downtown, and a slightly more upscale feel. Very popular with Travis families.

15–20 min to base

Family-friendlySuburbanShopping

Dixon

A small agricultural town west of Travis with a quiet, rural atmosphere, affordable homes, and a tight-knit community. Great for families wanting space and a slower pace.

15–20 min to base

RuralAffordableFamily-friendly
Schools

Fairfield-Suisun Unified School District / Vacaville Unified

Vacaville Unified is generally the higher-performing district and draws many military families. Fairfield-Suisun Unified serves the areas closest to base and has active military liaison programs. Both districts offer strong support for military-connected students.

What families should know

  • Vacaville High School and Will C. Wood High School are well-regarded in Solano County
  • Fairfield-Suisun schools have robust programs for military-connected students
  • Travis Unified School District, on base, serves on-base housing families with small class sizes
Things to Do

Life around Travis AFB

Attraction

Jelly Belly Factory Tour

The Jelly Belly headquarters in Fairfield offers free factory tours, a candy store, and seasonal events. A fun family outing and a unique local landmark.

Outdoors

Napa Valley Wine Country

World-class wineries are just 30 minutes north in Napa and Sonoma. Tasting tours, scenic drives, and gourmet dining make this a top perk of Travis duty.

Attraction

San Francisco Day Trips

The Golden Gate Bridge, Fisherman's Wharf, Alcatraz, and Chinatown are all about 60 minutes southwest. One of the best weekend day-trip cities in the world.

Dining

Bud's Pub & Grill

A Fairfield staple with hearty American fare, craft beers, and a welcoming atmosphere. Known for burgers and a popular spot with Travis personnel.

Outdoors

Suisun Valley Farms & Orchards

Local farms in the Suisun Valley offer u-pick produce, farm stands, and seasonal festivals. A taste of California agriculture minutes from base.

Outdoors

Sacramento River Delta

The nearby delta offers world-class bass fishing, kayaking, boating, and scenic waterway exploration. A hidden gem for outdoor enthusiasts.
