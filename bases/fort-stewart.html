Schools

Liberty County School System

Liberty County Schools serves Hinesville and surroundings, with heavy military enrollment and strong transition support programs.

What families should know

  • Liberty County High School and Bradwell Institute are the main campuses
  • Bryan County Schools (Richmond Hill) consistently outperforms Liberty County in state rankings
  • District has a full-time Military Family Liaison
Things to Do

Life around Fort Stewart

Attraction

Fort Stewart Museum

On-post museum documenting the 3rd Infantry Division's history — the oldest continuously active division in the U.S. Army.

Attraction

Savannah Historic District

One of America's most beautiful historic cities, 45 minutes from base — squares, antebellum architecture, River Street dining, and ghost tours.

Outdoors

Tybee Island Beach

Georgia's closest beach to Fort Stewart, about an hour away via Savannah — a beloved weekend getaway for base families.

Entertainment

Dogwood Festival & Coastal Georgia Events

Hinesville hosts multiple community festivals throughout the year serving the military family population.

Shopping

Oglethorpe Mall (Savannah)

Main regional mall in Savannah serving the Fort Stewart area with major retailers and a movie theater.

Outdoors

LeConte-Woodmanston Botanical Garden

Restored 18th-century plantation and botanical garden in nearby Riceboro — a peaceful piece of Georgia's natural and cultural history.
