Schools

Bossier Parish School System

Bossier Parish Schools is considered one of the stronger school systems in Louisiana, frequently outperforming neighboring Caddo Parish. Haughton and Airline Drive corridor schools are particularly well-regarded.

What families should know

  • Parkway High School and Haughton High School are top performers in the parish
  • Strong JROTC programs aligned with the base's military community
  • Multiple magnet and academy programs offering specialized tracks
Things to Do

Life around Barksdale AFB

Entertainment

Margaritaville Resort Casino

A large casino resort on the Red River with live entertainment, multiple dining options, and a hotel — a major local attraction.

Attraction

Barnwell Garden & Art Center

A serene botanical garden and sculpture park in Shreveport with seasonal flower displays and rotating art installations.

Shopping

Louisiana Boardwalk Outlets

An outdoor shopping and entertainment complex on the Red River in Bossier City with national retailers, restaurants, and a movie theater.

Dining

Waffle House at Barksdale Blvd

A beloved late-night institution for the military community — the Waffle House near base is a rite of passage for incoming troops.

Dining

Red River District

Shreveport's arts and entertainment district with locally owned restaurants, live music venues, and bars along the riverfront.

Outdoors

Cypress Black Bayou Recreation Area

A Bossier Parish park with a lake, camping, fishing, a zoo, and an aquarium — ideal for family outdoor recreation.
