Schools

Houston County School District

Houston County Schools is one of Georgia's top-performing large districts and the primary choice for Robins families. It serves a community with a high percentage of college-educated military and defense industry families.

What families should know

  • Northside High School is among Georgia's top large public schools with strong academics and athletics
  • Houston County's graduation rate and test scores consistently lead the state
  • Warner Robins High School and Veteran's High School offer competitive academic environments
Things to Do

Life around Robins AFB

Attraction

Museum of Aviation

One of the US Air Force's four official museums with 90+ aircraft including rare aircraft like the SR-71, B-1B, and U-2, free admission.

Entertainment

Georgia National Fairgrounds

The Perry fairgrounds host the annual Georgia National Fair each October plus major livestock events, concerts, and motorsports throughout the year.

Outdoors

Rum Creek WMA

A state wildlife management area north of Macon with hunting, fishing, hiking, and paddling on the Ocmulgee River.

Dining

Nu-Way Weiners

A central Georgia institution since 1916 serving the legendary crimson-colored Nu-Way hot dog in Macon — a must-try for any new arrival to the area.

Attraction

Ocmulgee Mounds National Historical Park

A significant Native American site in Macon with massive earthen mounds and a unique earthlodge dating back 1,000 years.

Outdoors

Lake Tobesofkee

A popular recreational lake west of Macon with boating, swimming, fishing, and camping facilities — a Robins family staple for summer.
