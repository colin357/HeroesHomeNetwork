Schools

Great Falls Public Schools

GFPS serves the Malmstrom community. The district is modestly sized and offers standard Montana public school programming. C.M. Russell High School and Great Falls High School are the two main secondaries.

What families should know

  • C.M. Russell High School offers strong fine arts programs reflecting the city's namesake artist
  • Great Falls College MSU provides community college dual enrollment
  • District has strong vocational-technical programs
Things to Do

Life around Malmstrom AFB

Outdoors

Giant Springs State Park

One of the largest freshwater springs in the US in Great Falls with a fish hatchery, Lewis & Clark interpretive exhibits, and riverside trails.

Attraction

C.M. Russell Museum

A world-class Western art museum dedicated to Charles M. Russell, the 'Cowboy Artist,' with original oils, bronzes, and his log-cabin studio.

Outdoors

River's Edge Trail

A 60+ mile trail system along the Missouri River in Great Falls offering cycling, running, and wildlife viewing through the city.

Dining

Bert & Ernie's Restaurant

A Great Falls favorite for casual American dining with excellent burgers, sandwiches, and a family-friendly atmosphere since the 1970s.

Outdoors

Glacier National Park

One of America's crown jewel national parks is about 2 hours north with the Going-to-the-Sun Road, alpine hiking, and stunning glaciated peaks.

Outdoors

Sluice Boxes State Park

A rugged canyon park south of Great Falls with hiking, fishing on Belt Creek, and dramatic limestone formations.

Buying or renting near Malmstrom AFB

Great Falls is one of Montana's most affordable cities. The housing market is stable and BAH covers costs well. Limited new construction but solid inventory of older homes. Very low competition compared to Bozeman or Missoula.
