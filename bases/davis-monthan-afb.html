Schools

Tucson Unified School District

TUSD is large and varied in quality by school. Many DM families opt for the Vail School District (southeast Tucson/Civano area) which is consistently rated among Arizona's best.

What families should know

  • Vail School District serves the Civano/Rita Ranch area and is an A-rated Arizona district
  • Empire High School in Vail was an early 1:1 laptop school and maintains strong academics
  • University High School (TUSD magnet) is one of Arizona's top public high schools
Things to Do

Life around Davis-Monthan AFB

Outdoors

Saguaro National Park East

Thousands of towering saguaro cacti with excellent hiking, cycling, and wildlife viewing just minutes from DM's east side.

Attraction

Pima Air & Space Museum

One of the world's largest aviation museums adjacent to DM's famous aircraft boneyard, with over 400 aircraft on display.

Dining

Fourth Avenue

Tucson's eclectic street of independent restaurants, bars, vintage clothing, and music venues — the heart of the city's alternative culture.

Outdoors

Mount Lemmon / Ski Valley

A 9,157-ft sky island accessible by a scenic highway with hiking, skiing in winter, and a charming mountain village.

Attraction

Tucson Gem & Mineral Show

The world's largest gem and mineral show held every February, transforming Tucson into a global destination for collectors and rockhounds.

Dining

El Charro Café

America's oldest family-operated Mexican restaurant, credited with inventing the chimichanga, serving Tucson since 1922.
