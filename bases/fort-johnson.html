Fort Johnson

Schools

Vernon Parish School Board

Vernon Parish schools serve Leesville and surrounding communities. The district is familiar with military family transitions given Fort Johnson's large training population.

What families should know

  • Leesville High School serves the primary base community
  • New Llano Elementary is popular with military families on the east side of Leesville
  • Beauregard Parish School Board (DeRidder area) has well-regarded schools for those willing to commute
Things to Do

Life around Fort Johnson

Outdoors

Toledo Bend Reservoir

One of the largest man-made lakes in the US on the Texas-Louisiana border, famous for world-class bass fishing and beautiful scenery.

Outdoors

Kisatchie National Forest

Louisiana's only national forest, surrounding Fort Johnson with hundreds of miles of hiking and ATV trails.

Dining

The Big Easy Bar & Grill

Local Leesville staple serving Louisiana classics like crawfish, boudin, and po'boys — authentic Cajun flavors near base.

Outdoors

Hodges Gardens State Park

Scenic garden park with a large lake, manicured gardens, and abundant wildlife — a peaceful escape from base life.

Attraction

Fort Polk Military Museum

On-post museum (former name) documenting the history of Fort Johnson and its predecessor installations since WWII.

Entertainment

Leesville Cinema

Local movie theater serving the Leesville community — one of the primary entertainment options in the small city.

Fort Johnson's market is among the most affordable in the Army. The remote location of Leesville keeps prices very accessible, though the rental market is heavily tied to military tenants.
