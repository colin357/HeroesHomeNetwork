Schools

Arlington Public Schools

Arlington Public Schools is one of the highest-rated urban school districts in the nation with diverse, well-resourced campuses and strong special programs.

What families should know

  • Washington-Lee (now Washington-Liberty) and Yorktown High School are the flagship high schools
  • District has numerous IB, language immersion, and STEM magnet programs
  • Very high teacher quality and significant per-pupil spending compared to national averages
Things to Do

Life around Fort Myer

Attraction

Arlington National Cemetery

America's most hallowed military burial ground — the Tomb of the Unknown Soldier and burial services are deeply meaningful experiences for military families.

Attraction

Pentagon Memorial

Outdoor memorial honoring the 184 victims of the 9/11 Pentagon attack — a moving and important site adjacent to the installation.

Shopping

Crystal City Underground

Underground shopping and dining corridor connecting Crystal City's office buildings — convenient for quick errands during the workday.

Dining

The Capital Grille (Tysons)

Upscale steakhouse in nearby Tysons Corner — ideal for officer dining and special occasions.

Outdoors

Theodore Roosevelt Island

Car-free island nature park in the Potomac River, accessible via footbridge — a surprising natural escape minutes from the DC hustle.

Attraction

Washington DC National Mall

The full suite of Smithsonian museums, monuments, and memorials — all free and within 15 minutes of Fort Myer.

Housing Market

Buying or renting near Fort Myer

Home Prices

$650,000–$1,200,000

Typical Rent

$2,500–$4,200/mo

Arlington is one of the most expensive housing markets in the country. Fort Myer assignments come with the highest BAH rates in the Army but purchasing is extremely challenging. Most junior personnel rent; senior officers may consider further-out suburbs.

Popular neighborhoods: Clarendon, Ballston, Columbia Pike
