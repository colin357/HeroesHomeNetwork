Schools

Geary County Schools USD 475

USD 475 serves Junction City and is one of the most military-connected districts in Kansas, with decades of experience supporting PCS family transitions.

What families should know

  • Junction City High School and Milford High School are the main campuses
  • District has one of the highest percentages of military-connected students in Kansas
  • Purple Star school designations district-wide
Things to Do

Life around Fort Riley

Attraction

First Infantry Division Museum

On-post museum honoring the Big Red One's history — an impressive collection of artifacts and vehicles spanning WWI to recent conflicts.

Outdoors

Milford Lake

Kansas's largest reservoir, just north of the base, with extensive recreation including sailing, fishing, hiking, and a state park.

Dining

Aggieville (Manhattan, KS)

The entertainment district adjacent to KSU with dozens of bars, restaurants, and live music venues — a popular off-post destination.

Outdoors

Konza Prairie Biological Station

Pristine native tallgrass prairie preserve near Manhattan with hiking trails offering an authentic Great Plains landscape experience.

Shopping

Manhattan Town Center Mall

Main regional mall in Manhattan with major retailers and dining, serving the Fort Riley and KSU community.

Attraction

Wamego Tulip Festival / Wizard of Oz Museum

Quirky small-town attraction in nearby Wamego celebrating Oz history and hosting an annual spring tulip festival.
