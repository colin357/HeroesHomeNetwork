Seymour Johnson AFB

Air Force Goldsboro, North Carolina

Off-Base Living

Top neighborhoods near Seymour Johnson AFB

Goldsboro (West Side)

The western neighborhoods of Goldsboro closest to base offer affordable single-family homes, convenient shopping, and quick gate access. A practical choice for E-5 and below.

5–10 min to base

AffordableMilitary communitySuburban

Pikeville / Mar-Mac

Unincorporated communities north of Goldsboro with larger lots, newer construction, and a rural feel. Popular with families wanting more space and privacy.

15–20 min to base

RuralFamily-friendlyNew construction

Mount Olive / Dudley

Small towns south and east of base with very affordable housing, farmland, and a tight-knit community atmosphere. A budget-friendly option for junior enlisted.

15–25 min to base

RuralAffordable
Schools

Wayne County Public Schools

Wayne County schools serve the Goldsboro area and have seen investments in STEM programs and facility upgrades. Several schools have active military family liaison programs due to the base presence.

What families should know

  • Eastern Wayne High School and Goldsboro High School offer AP and CTE programs
  • Strong JROTC programs with Air Force connections
  • Wayne School of Engineering (magnet) offers STEM-focused curriculum
Things to Do

Life around Seymour Johnson AFB

Outdoors

Cliffs of the Neuse State Park

A scenic state park along the Neuse River with swimming, hiking, and striking 90-foot cliffs — one of the geological gems of eastern North Carolina.

Dining

Wilber's Barbecue

A legendary eastern NC whole-hog barbecue joint in Goldsboro since 1962. Vinegar-based sauce and pit-cooked pork make it a must-stop for newcomers.

Outdoors

Seymour Johnson Golf Course

An on-base 18-hole course open to military and dependents, well-maintained and very affordable — one of the best MWR golf values in the Air Force.

Entertainment

Downtown Goldsboro Arts District

A revitalized downtown with galleries, murals, craft breweries, and the historic Paramount Theatre hosting live music and community events.

Dining

Grady's Barbecue

Another acclaimed whole-hog pit in nearby Dudley, often ranked among the top BBQ spots in North Carolina. Cash only and worth the trip.

Outdoors

Cherry Hospital Recreation Trail

A paved multi-use trail near Goldsboro popular with runners, cyclists, and families. Connects to local greenway extensions.
