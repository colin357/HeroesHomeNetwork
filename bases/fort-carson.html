Schools

Fountain-Fort Carson School District 8 / Colorado Springs District 11

Fountain-Fort Carson SD 8 serves the area immediately surrounding the base and is accustomed to high military family turnover with strong transition support.

What families should know

  • Fountain-Fort Carson High School has strong JROTC and athletic programs
  • District 8 maintains a dedicated Military Family Services coordinator
  • Academy District 20 (north Springs) is highly rated if commute is acceptable
Things to Do

Life around Fort Carson

Outdoors

Garden of the Gods

Stunning free public park with towering red rock formations and hiking trails — one of Colorado Springs' most iconic attractions.

Outdoors

Pikes Peak (America's Mountain)

Iconic 14,115-ft summit accessible by cog railway, toll road, or on foot — a bucket-list experience for every Fort Carson family.

Dining

Old Chicago Pizza & Taproom

Local Colorado chain with a massive beer selection and deep-dish pizza, popular gathering spot for soldiers and families.

Attraction

Cheyenne Mountain Zoo

America's only mountain zoo, perched at 6,714 feet with over 750 animals and incredible views of the Front Range.

Shopping

First & Main Town Center

Large outdoor shopping and dining complex on the Powers Corridor with dozens of national retailers and restaurants.

Entertainment

Manitou Springs

Quirky historic town at the foot of Pikes Peak with mineral springs, art galleries, and the famous Penny Arcade — a great day trip.
