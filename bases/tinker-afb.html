Off-Base Living

Top neighborhoods near Tinker AFB

Midwest City

The city directly adjacent to Tinker AFB with a wide range of housing from affordable to mid-range. Rose State College and a strong retail corridor make it the default choice for many Tinker families.

Schools

Mid-Del Schools / Moore Public Schools

Mid-Del Schools serve Midwest City and Del City directly near base. Moore Public Schools, further south, are generally higher-rated and draw many military families willing to commute. Norman Public Schools near OU are also excellent.

What families should know

  • Moore Public Schools consistently rank among the top districts in the OKC metro
  • Mid-Del Schools have strong military family support programs and JROTC
  • Norman Public Schools offer International Baccalaureate and advanced STEM programs
Things to Do

Life around Tinker AFB

Attraction

Oklahoma City National Memorial & Museum

A powerful memorial honoring the 168 victims of the 1995 bombing. The reflecting pool, empty chairs, and Survivor Tree are profoundly moving.

Entertainment

Bricktown District

OKC's revitalized entertainment district with restaurants, bars, a riverwalk, minor-league baseball, and a comedy club. The heart of OKC nightlife.

Outdoors

Lake Thunderbird State Park

A large reservoir between Norman and Midwest City with boating, fishing, swimming, hiking, and mountain biking trails. A popular weekend escape.

Dining

Cattlemen's Steakhouse

An Oklahoma City institution in Stockyards City since 1910, serving legendary steaks in a historic setting. A must-visit for any carnivore at Tinker.

Entertainment

OU Football at Gaylord Family Stadium

Watching the Sooners play on a fall Saturday in Norman is a quintessential Oklahoma experience. Military appreciation games offer discounted tickets.

Outdoors

Martin Park Nature Center

A 140-acre urban nature preserve in northwest OKC with trails through prairie, woodland, and wetland habitats. Free admission and great for families.
